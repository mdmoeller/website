I am an Assistant Professor in the Mathematics and Computer Science Department at the College of the Holy Cross. I completed my PhD in Computer Science at Cornell University in 2026, where I was co-advised by Nate Foster and Alexandra Silva.

My research is in programming languages, with a focus on automata learning and verifying network forwarding planes. See my research page for publications.

This fall I'm teaching Computer Organization and Design (CSCI 226) and Analysis of Algorithms (CSCI 235).

Holy Cross students: if you're interested in doing research in programming languages or formal methods, it would be great to chat about this! Feel free to reach out by email or stop by my office (Swords 332).
