I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at the College of the Holy Cross. I completed my PhD in Computer Science at Cornell University in 2026, where I was co-advised by Nate Foster and Alexandra Silva.

While I was a grad student, I enjoyed being an organizer of the Programming Languages Discussion Group (PLDG/CS7190) for several years. In August 2025, I organized the Upstate PL Seminar at Cornell.
